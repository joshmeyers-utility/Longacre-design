# Direction 03: Since 1969

Ragged Edge round (brief: `research/ragged-edge.md`). Mood board:
`directions/moodboards/03-since-1969.html` (shared emblem script
`03-since-1969-seal.js`), captured at deviceScaleFactor 2 as
`moodboards/03-since-1969-board.jpg` (1440 CSS px wide, full page) and
`moodboards/03-since-1969-375.jpg` (375, hero and stats, from
`03-since-1969-375.html`).

## Concept

The relaunch of an old firm. This site has made power since 1969: the Homer
City Generating Station ran from 1969 to 2023, and the Homer City Energy
Campus is going up on the same ground now. The direction treats that as one
long story and gives it the kit that Ragged Edge gave Globe-Trotter and
Papier: a seal, a refined serif in caps broken by lower-case italics, navy
and gold at full strength, and the brand applied to real objects. It is
heritage reinvigorated, not nostalgia. The past is a dated fact in the
sequence, not a sepia mood.

> **Positioning:** A generating station from 1969. A campus under
> construction now. One place, one long story, and every figure in it
> signed and dated.

**Voice test:** no other energy company can put 1969, Indiana County and
the former Homer City Generating Station in one sentence. Swap the name and
the line stops being true.

and a heavy italic sans. 02 is grotesque-led with a serif italic accent. 04
is a giant condensed sans. 05 is dark with a moving colour field. 03 is the
only direction led by a serif and built on an emblem system. It differs from
the rejected "The Record" (light Fraunces, gold hairlines, nothing loud) in
scale and fields: 250px caps, full gold and navy spreads, and a seal.

## Typography

Both faces are Google Fonts and port to Webflow.

| Role | Face | Weight | 375 | 1440 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display headline | Cormorant, caps; lower-case italic for the human phrase | 500 / italic 500 | 42 / 0.98 | 94 hero, 108–116 statement |
| Stat figure | Cormorant, `lining-nums` | 500 | 84 | 190 |
| Qualifier | Cormorant italic, gold | 500 | 22 | 34 |
| Stamp / seal | Cormorant caps, +12% tracking | 600 | 13–15 | 13–15 |
| Body | Instrument Sans | 400 / 600 | 17 | 18; lede 21 |
| Footnote | Instrument Sans | 400 | 12.5 | 13 |
| Eyebrow | Instrument Sans, sentence case, +4% | 600 | 12 | 13 |

Candidates rendered side by side: Cormorant, Playfair Display, Instrument
Serif, Bodoni Moda, DM Serif Display and Marcellus. Cormorant won on caps
refinement (closest to Papier) and has the best italic for the human
phrases. Playfair's caps read as wedding stationery. Instrument Serif is too
condensed, and 02 uses that register. Bodoni Moda's hairlines break up on
navy. **Cormorant's default figures are old-style**, so "~1,000" reads as
small caps. `font-variant-numeric: lining-nums` is set on the body and is
not optional.

**Departs from root §7:** all-caps display. Caps apply only through
`text-transform` on display sizes, so the source text stays sentence case
for screen readers and the CMS. Eyebrows, buttons and labels stay sentence
case.

## The emblem system

The logo is fixed and used as-is. The board shows it as a dashed "Logo
as-is · client vector" placeholder. Everything below is **secondary** and
sits beside the logo, never inside it, at no more than the logo's height.

- **The seal.** Ring: "HOMER CITY ENERGY CAMPUS" / "INDIANA COUNTY ·
  PENNSYLVANIA". Centre: the seven-ray mark on a horizon line, over
  "19 ✦ 69". The date is sourced: the station produced power on this site
  from 1969 (`iup-deck.md` photo essay "1969–2023"; QA history copy). No
  "Est." or "Rebuilt" date is claimed, because the sources give none.
- **The seven-ray mark.** One ray for each of the 7 × GE Vernova 7HA.02
  turbines. It works alone at billboard scale (the gold-field spread).
- **Fact variants.** The ring can carry one sourced fact ("UP TO 4.4 GW" /
  "7 × GE VERNOVA 7HA.02"). Trade stickers carry a trade name and
  "20 ✦ 26". The qualifier ("Up to") always travels with the figure.
- **Stamps that do factual work:** a milestone stamp ("First steel · April
  2026 · Vertical construction"); the "As of September 2026" pill that goes
  with every moving figure; and **Kind stamps** inside the image, top left:
  "Photograph · {Month Year}", "Rendering — not a photograph" (gold), and
  "Archival photograph · 1969–2023".

## Voice: two headlines rewritten from fact-sheet lines

Facts unchanged. Caps carry the claim and the italic is the human part.

1. Fact sheet: "Crossed first steel threshold with commencement of vertical
   construction" → **FIRST *steel.*** *April 2026.* Vertical construction
   has begun. (The date comes from the deck timeline. See the caveat below.)
2. Fact sheet: "Hosted first community open house, convening hundreds of
   residents from across Indiana County" → **THE FIRST OPEN HOUSE, *with
   hundreds of our neighbours.*** *July 2025.* Residents came from across
   Indiana County.

The locked hero is unchanged: "Building America's *energy future* by drawing
on Pennsylvania's *proud past.*" It is the fact sheet's positioning line, so
the direction's whole idea is already in the client's own words.

## Imagery

- **The photo essay is the spine.** The IUP deck's sequence (1969–2023 ·
  Mar 2025 · Nov 2025 · Feb 2026 · Mar 2026 · Apr 2026 · July 2026), with
  the deck's own captions, runs as a dated strip. Each date is set in
  Cormorant caps over a rule.
- **Archival frames sit on navy, new frames on cream.** The Kind stamp says
  which, inside the image.
- **Before and after from the same viewpoint.** The brief for Nov 2025 asks
  for the cleared skyline shot from the archival frame's position.
- **Renderings** (for example the deck cover) carry the gold "Rendering — not
  a photograph" stamp inside the image area, where no crop removes it.
- **Stand-ins.** The board uses `content/imagery/07` and `13` only as
  stand-ins, labelled inside the image ("Stand-in — suspected generated
  image, not a photograph of the campus") and never dated. Every dated frame
  is a briefed placeholder with its brief inside the image area.
- **Do not:** sepia, duotone or "aged" treatments on archival frames. The
  age shows in the date, not in a filter.

## The receipt

On a navy ledger, one row is one unit. The qualifier sits above the figure
in gold Cormorant italic (*Anticipated*, *Up to*). The figure is set at
190px in Cormorant lining figures, with the footnote numeral attached as a
gold italic superior. The right-hand column holds the label in Instrument
Sans and, directly beneath it, the footnote verbatim in 13px with a matching
gold italic numeral. Moving figures carry the "As of September 2026" pill,
which includes a miniature seal, so a date reads as a stamp rather than as
fine print. The same row works at 375 (84px figure) and on the 1080 social
card, where footnote 1 is part of the card itself.

Figures shown: 1,800+ (as of September 2026, CMS field; tier A wording to
confirm), 10,000+ with footnote 1, ~1,000 with footnote 2, and Up to
4.4 GW.

## Motion and Webflow feasibility

- **Seal reveal:** the rays draw from the horizon one by one, 7 × 60ms
  stagger, `ease-out` over 600ms. This uses Interactions 2.0 scroll-into-view
  on seven SVG paths, so it needs no GSAP.
- **Caps and italic:** the headline fades and rises 12px (300ms). The italic
  phrase follows 120ms later. This is native.
- **Photo-essay strip:** horizontal scroll-progress moves a gold rule along
  the dates. This is native scroll-progress.
- **Figures never count up.** A figure that animates reads as spin.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: everything renders in its final state.
- **Build notes:**
  - The seal and mark ship as static SVG files. `03-since-1969-seal.js` only
    draws them for the mood board.
  - `lining-nums` is a custom property, so it goes in the site-wide custom
    code `<style>`.
  - Text on a curved path (the seal ring) stays inside the SVG, never live
    text.

## Brand in the world (drawn on the board)

1. **Site gate sign, Power Plant Road:** an enamelled navy panel on posts,
   with the gold seal, "HOMER CITY *Energy Campus*", the address and two
   contact routes. It carries no access wording until open question #11 is
   answered.
2. **Fact-sheet cover:** "QUICK *facts*" on cream stock, with the fan mark in
   gold, a blind-embossed seal in print, and the edition date (May 2026).
3. **Social card, 1080²:** 10,000+ with *Anticipated* and footnote 1
   verbatim on the card.
4. **Commemorative "First *steel*" poster:** gold field, navy type, April
   2026, with the deck timeline wording. **Caveat (root §10 #12):** the
   deck's photo essay dates the photograph to March 2026, while its timeline
   dates the milestone to April 2026. The poster and stamp use April, and
   the essay keeps March.
5. **Construction-update email header:** navy band with the seal and
   "CONSTRUCTION *update*". The body copy is the mobile alert ("Heavy
   equipment transport… begins September 16.").
6. **Hard-hat stickers:** one per trade (trade names only; the nine unions
   are not named, open question #2).

## What it avoids

- **Nostalgia kitsch:** no sepia, no smokestack silhouettes, no "good old
  days". The station appears as a dated entry, not as a mood.
- **Fake textures:** no paper grain, rust or distressed stamps on screen.
  Emboss and foil are used only where there is real paper.
- **The word "coal", and 4.5 GW:** neither appears. The site is "the former
  Homer City Generating Station", and the figure is up to 4.4 GW.
- **Seal as logo:** the seal never replaces, recolours or crowds the fixed
  logo, and never carries a date the sources don't give.

## Doubts

- **"Since 1969" implies continuity.** Power generation stopped in 2023. The
  seal and the name read as "this site's story since 1969", but a sceptic
  could read them as a claim that power never stopped. Josh or the client
  may prefer the seal centre to read "1969 ✦ 2026".
- **Navy plus gold plus a seal sits close to institutional or collegiate
  crest territory.** Scale and the gold fields keep it from being polite,
  but at small sizes it can look like a bank.
- **Cormorant's thin strokes:** below about 20px on navy they break up.
  Stamps use weight 600, and nothing below 13px is set in the serif.
- **The eight-line 375 headline** is long. It is legible, but it pushes the
  hero image below the fold.
