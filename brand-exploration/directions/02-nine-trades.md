## 1. Concept

**Positioning line:** *The Homer City Energy Campus, credited to the nine
trades building it.*

The workforce is the identity. Boilermakers, carpenters, electricians,
ironworkers, laborers, millwrights, operators, pipefitters and teamsters
are shown one trade at a time, each against its own colour field, captioned
by trade in a soft serif italic and set beside a heavy grotesque that states
the facts. The site reads as a credit roll before it reads as a project.

**Voice test.** "Nine trades", "~95% of the skilled direct-hire craft
workforce is from the local area", "135+ apprentices training alongside
experienced journeymen" and "9 local union organizations" are only true
here. Swap the name and the sentence stops being true.

**How it differs from the superseded 04 Crew:** Crew was navy-led, duotone,
condensed and documentary. Nine Trades is cream-led and warm, uses four
colour fields for portraits (Tilt / Marshmallow), and its signature is the
grotesque + serif-italic mix, not a condensed face.

**Distinct from the parallel directions:** 01 owns one colour field and a
heavy italic wordmark; 03 is a heritage serif; 04 is giant condensed type;
05 is dark with a moving field. 02 is the only one whose hero subject is
faces, and the only one whose voice lives in a serif italic *inside* a
grotesque line.

## 3. Typography

Chosen by rendering Hanken Grotesk 900, Schibsted Grotesk 900, Familjen
Grotesk 700 and Bricolage Grotesque 800 against Instrument Serif, Fraunces
italic and Newsreader italic. Schibsted 900 has Tilt's dense, slightly
compressed weight; Fraunces italic (opsz 144, SOFT 50) is warmer and rounder
than Instrument Serif, which keeps it far from a stencil or a contractor
sign.

| Role | Font (Google) | Weight | 1440 | 375 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display (H1) | Schibsted Grotesk | 900, −0.04em, lh 0.9 | 112 | 46 |

| H2 | Schibsted Grotesk | 900 | 84 | 36 |

Rule: **one italic phrase per heading, and it is the people** (the trade,
"tradesmen and women", "Pennsylvania's proud past"), never a product or an
adjective. Sentence case everywhere; no all-caps.

### Staying clear of Kiewit

Kiewit's yellow and black is on the workers' own hard hats and vests, and
Tilt's yellow/black is close to it. How 02 avoids it:

- **No yellow on black, and no black at all.** The darkest value is navy.
- **Gold is a muted ochre** (#D8B471), not a safety yellow, and it only ever
  carries navy type. It is never the lead field: one of four portrait
  fields, plus the jobs header.
- **No squared caps, stencils, hazard stripes or chevrons.** The brand word
  is a soft serif italic in sentence case, the opposite of signage.
- **Stickers and patches** sit on the contractor's vest and hat as a navy,
  aqua or cream mark; we never recolour or imitate the contractor's kit.

## 5. Voice

Plain, warm, specific. Credit people by trade; let the number be the
compliment. Two sample headlines rewritten from fact-sheet lines, facts
unchanged (proposals; the locked copy in the mood board is untouched):

- Fact sheet: "Mobilized construction workforce with Kiewit and local
  unions with 1,000+ workers active on site" → "*Local unions* put
  1,000+ workers on site with Kiewit."
- Fact sheet: "Safety is every HCG employee's #1 priority; we are accountable
  for our actions and responsive to concerns." → "For *every* Homer City
  Generation employee, safety comes first. We answer for it, and we answer
  your concerns."

## 6. Imagery

- **Portrait recipe:** one person, chest-up, in their own work gear, looking
  to camera or just off it. Shot on site against a painted seamless or sheet
  in the trade's field colour (gold, aqua, navy, cream), so a mixed library
  reads as one series. Soft morning or late side light; real skin, real
  wear. No retouching of dirt or PPE.
- **Captions name the trade only** until a name is supplied with consent.
- **Action frames:** apprentice and journeyman side by side at the work face;
  not posed. Full bleed, once per page.
- **Placeholders:** a lit, empty set in the field colour, with the brief
  inside the image area (as in the board). Never a silhouette or avatar.
- **Stand-ins:** the 32 supplied images are suspected generated; where used
  they carry "Stand-in — suspected generated image, not a photograph of the
  campus" inside the image, and none showing people is used.
- **Renderings:** a "Rendering" label in the same position as the stand-in
  label (cream plate, navy type, top-left inside the image), plus
  `Kind = Rendering` in the CMS.

## 7. Motion and Webflow

- **Trade tile rollover:** one state for hover, keyboard focus and tap. The
  portrait scales 1.03 (240 ms, `ease-appear` cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)); the
  description, already in the DOM at rest, fades up. Tile is a link/button
  with a 3 px link-blue focus ring. Interactions 2.0: hover + click
  triggers on a Collection item; focus via `:focus-visible` style. Reduced
  motion: no scale, instant reveal.
- **Portrait strip:** horizontal scroll (native overflow, scroll-snap).
- **Figures:** scroll-into-view fade-up only; no counting animation (a
  counting number is briefly a wrong number).
- **CMS:** Trades collection (name, description, field colour option,
  portrait, kind, alt, union local optional); Stats collection with
  qualifier, figure, label, as-of date, footnote, tier flag.
- Fonts: both in Webflow's Google list. Fraunces axes (opsz, SOFT) need a
  small custom-code style; fallback is the default instance.

## 9. The receipt

Every figure is set as one piece of type: **qualifier** (only the source's
own word: *As of September 2026*, *Projected*, *Anticipated*) in gold or
navy Fraunces italic directly above; **figure** in Schibsted 900 with its
footnote numeral attached; **label** in Schibsted 500; **footnote** verbatim
in Fraunces beneath, inside the same rule. Tier-A workforce figures carry a
dashed "Tier A · confirm before publishing" flag until confirmed. The same
stack scales from a 150 px ledger to a 1080 social card.

## 10. What it avoids

Names and faces we do not have; invented eyebrows; yellow-on-black; squared
caps; stock smiles; mascots; gold as the lead field; counting-up numbers;
"coal"; 4.5 GW; any figure without its footnote or date.

## 11. Doubts

- Without real portraits this direction is half-built; it stands or falls on
  a commissioned shoot (open question #4) and consent for names.
- The QA trade descriptions for Operators ("Specializing in the construction
  industry") and Laborers look swapped; used verbatim, needs the client.
- Union roster (#2) is not public, so the board counts 9 but names none.
- Gold is close enough to a warm yellow that a careless printer could drift
  it; spec it as a Pantone and test on site next to Kiewit kit.
