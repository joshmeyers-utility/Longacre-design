Grading recipe

Light
Overcast-bright or high sun. No golden hour, no dusk: dusk belongs to other directions and to Kiewit.
Exposure
High-key: +0.3 to +0.5 stop. Highlights roll off to cream #F3F1D0, never blown to pure white.
Shadows
Lifted. Black point no darker than a navy-tinted #2A3A4C.
Contrast · saturation
Contrast −10. Saturation −15 to −20. Greens pulled toward olive and straw, so fields read as Pennsylvania farmland, not as “green energy”.
White balance
Neutral to a touch warm. Never pushed cyan or blue (no water or renewables reading).
Crop
Wide: 21:9 and 16:9 for place, horizon in the top third. People and detail: 4:5. One subject per frame.
CSS preview
brightness(1.1) contrast(0.86) saturate(0.62) sepia(0.12) hue-rotate(−8deg), plus a 16% haze screen to lift shadows, for comps only. Final grade is done on the files.

Tonal range of a graded frame: navy-tinted black point to cream white point. Illustrative, not sampled from a photograph.

What Daylight avoids

  • Green. No leaves, no wind or solar imagery, no green accent, no saturated fields.
  • Aqua as a ground. A pale-aqua band beside a water answer looks like a claim.
  • Blue small text. #238FC8 only at 64px and up.
  • Text over photographs. The headline sits on haze; the picture sits below.
  • Dusk and golden hour. The light is ordinary daylight.
  • Cards, tiles, pictograms. Hairlines and space only.
  • Ørsted’s lifestyle cast. No kitesurfers. Real, named neighbours.
  • Gradients and particle textures. They read as a tech vendor.
  • Count-up numbers. The figure is right from the first frame.
  • The current look. No navy hero, no Geist, no near-black figures.
