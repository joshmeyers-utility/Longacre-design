Sheet 00.3 · Type

One family, two widths, and a drafting hand.

Archivo's width axis does the work: expanded for display and figures (the lettering on a title block), normal width for reading. JetBrains Mono, not Plex, for annotation: it reads as instrument and drawing, not as editorial stamp.

Sheet 00.4 · Linework

Lines that point at something real.

Contours are abstracted rolling terrain, drawn schematic and labelled so: no elevations, no coordinates, no claim to be a map. The turbine sheet shows the count, not the layout. Grid references (A–F, 1–8) are the drawing's own, not geographic.

Sheet 00.7 · Plates

Photographs as plates, keyed in gold.

Warmth comes from the place: farms, woods and the town carry as much of the sheet as the steel does. Each plate has a number, a kind (Photograph, Rendering, or here Stand-in) and its date as separate fields; callouts name a location, never a spec.

Sheet 00.6 · Not this

What Survey refuses.

Blueprint clichéWhite lines on cyan-blue ground. Our drawing is navy ink on white; blue is the datum line, not the paper.

Invented map dataNo coordinates, elevations, north-arrowed plans or parcel lines the client hasn't supplied. Contours are labelled schematic.

Cold tech vendorNo dark-grid "technology" bands, extended caps or sunrise heroes (X-energy). Sentence case, real place, plain words.

Count-up figuresThe figure is drawn whole. Lines may draw on; numbers never animate.

Kiewit registerNo yellow-gold rule on navy with squared caps. Gold here is small mono annotation, never a band.

Decorative lineworkEvery leader ends at something: a photo feature, a note, a sheet. A line pointing at nothing is a lie in this system.
