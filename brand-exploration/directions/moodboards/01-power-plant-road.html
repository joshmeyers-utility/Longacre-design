Power
Plant Road.

What’s going up at 1750 Power Plant Road, in plain numbers, with the dates on.

One owned colour, one loud typeface, and the receipts set as big as the claims. Borrowed from Palmetto: energy made warm and plainly spoken, the brand out on the road. Translated for neighbours who drive past the gate every day.

The idea

Everyone in Indiana County knows the road. Fewer know what is going up at the end of it. This direction names the brand after the address and talks like a neighbour leaning on the fence: short, specific, a little dry, never selling.

The gold field is the brand. Navy is the voice. Every big number carries its own small print, set in the same loud type so nobody can say it was hidden.

Borrowed from Palmetto

The single saturated field, full-bleed. A heavy italic display that grins a little. A deep tonal field where the bright colour turns into type (their red on maroon, our gold on navy). The circled word, “with”, which becomes our circled date. The van, the patch, the billboard.

Colour

The client’s five colours, used as fields, not tints. Gold owns the brand the way Palmetto owns red. Navy is its tonal partner. Cream is for reading. Aqua is a spark, never a field. Blue appears only as illustration line and at display size.

Out on the road
