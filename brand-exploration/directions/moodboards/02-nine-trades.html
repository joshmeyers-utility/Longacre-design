Nine Trades

The Homer City Energy Campus is being built by boilermakers, carpenters, electricians, ironworkers, laborers, millwrights, operators, pipefitters and teamsters. This direction gives them the credit, by trade, with the figures beside them.

Portrait grid, nine trades. Every tile is a placeholder: the brief sits inside the image. Captions name the trade only and carry its locked description (content/homer-city-qa.md, Job opportunities). No person is named; none are supplied.

Define

A campus of 3,200+ acres is hard to picture from Homer City. A pipefitter from the next town over is not. So the identity is the people doing the work, shown by trade, each in their own colour, set in a heavy grotesque that says what it is and a soft serif italic that says who.

The figures stay with them. ~95% of the skilled direct-hire craft workforce is from the local area; 135+ apprentices train alongside experienced journeymen; 9 local union organizations are represented on site. Each number is set with its qualifier, its date and its footnote, in the same type, as one line of reading.

Not Kiewit’s colours

Gold field, navy type: yes
Navy field, gold italic: yes
Never: yellow on black, squared caps, hazard stripes
  • No yellow-on-black, no black at all. The darkest colour is navy #143251. Gold #D8B471 is a muted ochre (not a safety yellow) and carries navy type only.
  • No squared caps. Sentence case throughout; the brand word is a soft serif italic, the opposite of a stencil.
  • No gold as the lead field. Cream and navy lead; gold is one portrait field among four and the jobs header.
  • No names, no faces we don’t have. Captions by trade; portraits are briefs until real, consented photographs exist.
  • No hazard stripes, chevrons or stencils. Those belong to the site’s safety signage and the contractor.
