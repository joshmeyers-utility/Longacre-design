Direction 03 — Since 1969

Homer City committed to its own story. With a brand that carries a generating station from 1969 into a campus under construction.

Case-study chrome (wide grotesque, pills) is the presentation register, not part of the brand. The brand is everything below.
The campus seal. A secondary emblem, beside the logo, never instead of it. Seven rays, one for each GE Vernova 7HA.02 turbine in the Power Block, rising from one horizon. “19 ✦ 69” is the year the Homer City Generating Station began producing power on this site.

The Homer City Generating Station produced power on this site from 1969 to 2023. In April 2025, a plan was unveiled to build the Homer City Energy Campus in its place.

This direction treats that as one long story. Heritage reinvigorated, the way a good old firm is relaunched: a seal, a refined serif, navy and gold used at full strength. Not sepia. Not a museum.

Positioning
A generating station from 1969. A campus under construction now. One place, one long story — and every figure in it signed and dated.

Voice test. No other energy company can say 1969, Indiana County and the former Homer City Generating Station in one sentence. Swap the name and the line stops being true.

Drawn from

Globe-Trotter (re-globe-trotter f07, f10–f13): the crest with a date split by a mark (“18 ✦ 97”), gold on navy at full scale, the blind-embossed mark on cream, a refined serif headline with one italic word (“128 years and counting”).

Papier (re-papier f01, f04, f05, f09, f10, f13): the fan mark, set huge; refined caps interrupted by lower-case italics (“PURVEYORS of the POTENTIALLY REMARKABLE”); products shot on flat colour fields.

Nostalgia kitschNo sepia, no smokestack silhouettes, no “the good old days”. The station is a dated fact in the sequence, not a mood.
Fake textureNo paper grain, rust or distressed stamps on screen. Emboss and foil exist only where there is real paper.
The forbidden wordsNever “coal”. Never 4.5 GW. The site is “the former Homer City Generating Station”, and the figure is up to 4.4 GW.
Seal as logoThe seal never replaces, recolours or crowds the fixed logo, and never carries a date the sources don’t give.
