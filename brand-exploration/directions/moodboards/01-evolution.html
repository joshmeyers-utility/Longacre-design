Anatomy of the receipt
  1. Qualifier or date13px SemiBold, above the figure. Anticipated, Projected, Up to, or a navy-marked “As of September 2026”. An empty slot is kept so figures align.
  2. FigureGeist Light, navy #143251, tabular. 56px at 375, 68px at 1440. Never counts up.
  3. Label17/18px Regular. The superscript stays, so the pairing reads either way.
  4. FootnoteThe row’s fourth line, not fine print. 14/15px, line height 1.55, stone #55514C at 7.87:1, run-in “Footnote 1” in navy. Up from 12px grey at 4.7:1.
  5. Receipt lineThe source document by name and date, one baseline. A link, so it is blue; nothing else here is.
  6. The ruleA hairline closes the row. No card, no box, no tint. Same unit on a social card.
