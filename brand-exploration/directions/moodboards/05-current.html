Homer City Energy Campus

Power was made on this ground from 1969 to 2023. Current shows the same place switching back on: a field of colour that always converges on one address, 1750 Power Plant Rd, and never moves without a dated figure beside it.

Drawn from Ragged Edge’s own site (ultra-wide caps edge to edge, the full-bleed blurred field, serif pill navigation, serif long copy) and Granola (an electric accent on near-black, an oversized serif word as the playful moment). Here that word is as of.

Hero. Caps stay edge to edge at 30–44px. The H1 sits inside the navy band (.75 at 375 too; the band is taller so the H1 never leaves it).

Pills. Two on screen: the current page and the alert. The rest sit behind “Menu”, which opens a full-screen field with the pills stacked.

Numbers. Figure, then qualifier, then label, then footnote, all inside the row. The giant as of drops to 72px and keeps its colour.

Weight. The field ships as a 6–8 s looping H.264/WebM at 720p, ≈ 600 KB on mobile, behind a 20 KB poster. On Save-Data or slow connections only the poster loads.
