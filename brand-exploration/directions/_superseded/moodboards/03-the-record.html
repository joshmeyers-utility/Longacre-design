The Record

The record of what is being built on Power Plant Road, kept for the people who live beside it.

Concept. The site reads like a serious regional paper’s data desk: a light editorial serif, a warm paper ground, gold hairline rules. The trust is typographic. Every figure carries its qualifier, its note, its date and its document, set as one unit.

Still by default. Reduced motion is the look, not the fallback. One quiet fade, and only when the reader’s settings allow it.

Departs from the current design’s “no tinted grounds” rule: the page itself is paper, a lightened step of the client’s cream #F3F1D0. There are still no cards.

Avoids nostalgia. No newsprint texture, no blackletter, no dingbats or ornamental rules. A modern editorial site, not a 1950s paper.

Avoids Survey (05). Mono is a dateline and a citation, never a coordinate, grid label or dimension. Serif carries the voice.

Avoids spin. No count-ups, no scroll theatre, no adjectives in the figures. The figure arrives whole, with its note.
