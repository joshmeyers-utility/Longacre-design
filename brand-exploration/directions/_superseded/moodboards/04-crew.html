Crew

The Homer City Energy Campus, shown through the nine trades building it.

The workforce is the identity. Tradespeople photographed plainly on the job, graded to one duotone so a patchy library reads as one crew.

Navy ground, cream type, a tall condensed face in sentence case. Every number is a receipt.

No yellow, no black, no capitals: nothing a neighbour could mistake for the contractor’s own site.

Three portraits edge to edge, then the headline on solid navy. Nothing sits over a photograph, so contrast doesn’t depend on the picture.

The first receipt arrives directly below the buttons, already dated. The footnote is set at 15px inside the row, not at the foot of the page.
