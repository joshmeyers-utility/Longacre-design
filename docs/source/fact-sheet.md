## Positioning statement

> **Homer City Generation is building America's energy future by drawing on
> Pennsylvania's proud past.**
>
> In partnership with a hard-working community steeped in generations of energy
> expertise, Homer City Generation is transforming legacy energy infrastructure
> into a reliable provider of safe power and connectivity solutions. At a moment
> when America needs leadership and modern grid infrastructure, our team and
> partners have established a model that creates shared value for our neighbors
> and energy customers alike.

## Project timeline & key milestones

### 2025: Demolition & site development

| When | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| MAR | Demolition of the stacks and towers at the former Homer City Generating Station begins |
| APR | Plan unveiled to build the Homer City Energy Campus in partnership with Kiewit Power Constructors Co. |
| APR | GE Vernova confirmed to provide seven high-efficiency 7HA.02 natural gas turbines for the campus |
| APR | Corey Hessen appointed as CEO, bringing significant experience in power generation operations |
| JUN | Launch of Safety & Security Community Collaborative Team and Integrated Emergency Management Team |
| JUL | Hosted first community open house, convening hundreds of residents from across Indiana County |
| JUL | Participated in Senator McCormick's Inaugural Pennsylvania Energy & Innovation Summit |
| NOV | PA DEP approved of HCG's air quality plan, authorizing construction and initial operations |

### 2026–2027: Construction

- Mobilized construction workforce with Kiewit and local unions with 1,000+ workers active on site
- Crossed first steel threshold with commencement of vertical construction
- Completion of major demolition and transition into full construction phase
- Secured key permits and agreements for gas, EPC, and offtake operations
- Installation of first equipment, including first deliveries of GE Vernova turbines expected in 2026
- Anticipated announcement of customer(s)
