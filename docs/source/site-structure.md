## Brief

**Goal:** Provide transparency on the facts of the project, serve as an
information hub about the project, educate the community, correct misperceptions
about the project.

**Audience:** Local community members in Homer City / Indiana County PA who are
seeking information on the project.

**Problem with the current site:** Current website is outdated and feels too much
like a bland/generic corporate website — it isn't accessible/digestible for
community and doesn't spotlight the right information clearly.

**The pivot:** New website will be framed around the **Homer City Energy Campus**
— rather than Homer City Redevelopment (the company behind it). Put as much focus
as possible on the positives of the workforce / econ impact.
