## B — Power Plant & Fuel Supply, 9 Sep 2026

Header: *Homer City Energy Campus | Fact Sheet | September 2026*

### Power Plant & Fuel Supply

> **Homer City is building on its energy legacy.** The former coal plant site is
> being transformed into a modern energy campus designed to create jobs, attract
> major investment and use Pennsylvania natural gas to produce dependable power.

### The Power Plant At A Glance

| 4.4 GW | 7 | Early 2028 | Front of the Meter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Planned Capacity | Advanced Turbines | Power Expected | Power |

The Homer City Energy Campus will use seven GE Vernova 7HA.02 high-efficiency
natural gas turbines. At up to 4.4 gigawatts, the plant is designed to generate
more electricity than the campus' customer will need, enabling it to provide
dependable power for the campus while also supplying electricity to the local
grid.

### The Facts

- **A productive future for a legacy site.** As a brownfield site, the project
  reuses the old coal plant's site infrastructure including the existing
  transmission connections, substations and water infrastructure.
- **More efficient power generation.** The new natural gas-fired combined cycle
  units will be 40% more efficient than the former coal units and will emit 60%
  less carbon dioxide per megawatt-hour than the former coal plant.
- **Power in Front of the Meter.** All power produced on the Homer City Energy
  Campus will flow directly to the grid as a Front of the Meter transaction, and
  our customer will only take a portion of that power.
- **More than $10 billion in initial investment in the power plant.** Power
  infrastructure and site readiness will exceed $10 billion, with data center
  development expected to add billions more.
- **Built by experienced partners.** Kiewit Power Constructors is leading
  engineering, procurement and construction, and GE Vernova is supplying the
  turbines and combined-cycle equipment.

### Powering Homes and America's Digital Future

**The Homer City Energy Campus will have enough generating capacity** to serve
multiple on-site customers and to supply power to thousands of homes on the
local grid.

**The Homer City Energy Campus will help relieve pressure on the Mid-Atlantic
region**, which PJM — the nation's largest grid operator — predicts could face an
energy capacity deficiency by the end of this decade.

### Pennsylvania Gas. Two Supply Paths.

- **Reliable power begins with reliable fuel.** Homer City Generation's fuel
  plan is designed to connect the campus to two major interstate pipeline
  systems, providing more than one route for Pennsylvania-produced Marcellus
  natural gas to reach the plant.
- **Multiple supply paths available.** The plan can draw from both Texas Eastern
  Transmission and Eastern Gas Transmission and Storage, providing an alternate
  supply path if one source is constrained.
- **Two underground connections.** The site has two underground "lateral" lines
  that connect Homer City to the existing natural gas network serving the
  regions:
  - **Main Line:** A 20-mile pipeline from Delmont to Homer City
  - **Backup Line:** A 6-mile line from Blairsville to Homer City
- **A more efficient fuel source.** The new natural gas power plant will tap into
  a more efficient and environmentally responsible fuel source with natural gas
  from the Marcellus Shale delivered safely and quietly through underground
  pipelines.

### How It Works

The Power Plant at the heart of the Homer City Energy Campus is designed to
generate more electricity than our customer needs.

- The power is generated "in front of the meter," meaning it is all sold onto
  the public grid.
- Our customer will draw an agreed amount of power at an agreed price under a
  long-term contract known as a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA).
- Because the plant will produce more power than the customer consumes, the rest
  stays on the grid – creating a new supply of electricity for everyone in this
  region at a time when demand is growing fast.

Diagram: **Power plant** (Generates power) → **Public grid** (Metered delivery)
→ **Customer** (Contracted power defined in Power Purchase Agreement) and
**Local homes** (Remaining capacity).

### Frequently Asked Questions

1. **How much power will the plant generate compared to the old coal plant?**
   The former Homer City Generating Station generated approximately 1.8 GW when
   it was operational. By comparison, the new natural gas power plant is
   designed to generate 4.4 GW while emitting 60% less carbon dioxide per
   megawatt-hour.
2. **When will the plant be up and running?** The Homer City Energy Campus is
   currently under construction and is advancing toward its target of producing
   first power in early 2028.
3. **How much of the plant's power generation will go to your customer, and how
   much will be available for regular people who live and work around here?**
   The plant is designed to generate more electricity than our customer needs.
   All the power generated will be placed on the local grid, and our customer
   will only utilize an amount it has agreed to use under a long-term contract.
   The surplus power will be more than sufficient to supply electricity to
   thousands of homes and business throughout the region.
4. **As the campus grows, will it drive up my power bill?** Electricity rates are
   reviewed and approved by the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission, and we do
   not have a role in determining those rates for the community. However, the
   plant is designed to increase the availability of electricity for local homes
   and businesses, which relieves pressure on the grid. In addition, the plant
   will operate 40% more efficiently on a heat rate basis Net kW/MMbtu than the
   former coal plant.

## C — Economic Impact + Workforce, 24 Sep 2026

Header: *Homer City Energy Campus | Fact Sheet | September 2026*

### Economic Impact

> **By transforming the former coal plant into the state-of-the-art Homer City
> Energy Campus, this project is poised to bring an unprecedented level of
> economic opportunity to the region while returning thousands of jobs back to
> the local community.** As the largest natural gas power plant under
> construction in the nation and one of the largest capital investments in
> Pennsylvania's history, the project is a long-term investment in the
> Commonwealth.

Hero stat: **$10 Billion+** — Projected initial capital investment for power
infrastructure and site readiness¹

### Investment That Stays In Pennsylvania

- **Capital Investment:** An initial capital investment projected to exceed $10
  billion for power infrastructure and site readiness,¹ making this one of the
  largest such investments in Pennsylvania's history.
- **Jobs:** Creation of nearly 10,000 direct on-site construction-related jobs²
  along with approximately 1,000 total direct and indirect permanent high-paying
  positions³ in technology, operations and energy infrastructure.
- **Workforce Development & Training:** A pipeline of family-sustaining careers
  for both experienced tradespeople and the next generation of apprentices who
  are gaining hands-on training on the Homer City Energy Campus.
- **Tax and Other Revenue:** The Homer City Energy Campus will boost local school
  budgets, townships and the county through property taxes, as well as through
  taxes paid by the thousands of people who work to build and operate the site.
- **Business Community:** The Homer City Energy Campus is already generating
  significant economic activity throughout Indiana County and the surrounding
  region, supporting local businesses, restaurants and lodging providers while
  also engaging local partners on-site.
- **Local Power:** All power generated will be placed on the local grid, and our
  customer will only utilize a portion. The surplus power will be more than
  sufficient to supply electricity to thousands of homes and businesses
  throughout the region.
