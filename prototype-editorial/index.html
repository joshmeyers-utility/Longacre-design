In partnership with a hard-working community steeped in generations of energy expertise, Homer City Generation is transforming legacy energy infrastructure into a reliable provider of safe power and connectivity solutions.

From the Homer City Energy Campus fact sheetMay 2026

What is being built

A natural gas-powered campus of 3,200+ acres, designed to meet the needs of America’s digital future. Seven GE Vernova turbines are expected to produce up to 4.4 GW of energy on site.

Where

On the site of the former Homer City Generating Station, which produced power here from 1969 to 2023. The address is 1750 Power Plant Rd, Homer City.

What stage it is at

Major demolition finished in June 2026 and the site is now in full construction. First deliveries of GE Vernova turbines are expected in 2026.

Who we are

From past to present

The Homer City Generating Station produced power on this site from 1969 to 2023.

In April 2025, a plan was unveiled to build the Homer City Energy Campus in its place, in partnership with Kiewit Power Constructors Co. Demolition of the stacks and towers began the month before.
