The campus

Image brief. Power Block under construction, workers in frame

A natural gas power plant and data center campus, on the site of the former generating station.

The Homer City Energy Campus covers more than 3,200 acres in Indiana County. At its center are seven GE Vernova 7HA.02 natural gas turbines, and the campus is expected to produce up to 4.4 GW of energy on site. It is being built in partnership with Kiewit Power Constructors Co.

The site tour

Four stops on the site tour

The route visitors walk on a site tour, in order, with the dates each stop was photographed going up.

  1. Stop 1 of 4

    Power Block

    7 × GE Vernova 7HA.02 natural gas turbines

    The turbines burn natural gas to turn generators. Heat recovery steam generators (HRSGs) capture the heat in their exhaust and use it to make steam, which turns more generators.

    • April 2026Power Block aerial, looking north
    • July 2026Unit 5 HRSG

  2. Stop 2 of 4

    Gas insulated switchgear (GIS)

    Building and foundations

    Switchgear connects the plant to the power grid and protects it, switching and isolating high-voltage circuits. Gas insulated switchgear is sealed in an insulating gas, so it needs far less room than open-air equipment.

  3. Stop 3 of 4

    Gas compressor station

    Fuel supply

    Natural gas arrives by pipeline. The compressor station raises its pressure to the level the turbines need.
